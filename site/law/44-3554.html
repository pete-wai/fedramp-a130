44 USC §3554 — Federal agency responsibilities

Agency heads must protect information and systems in proportion to risk, assign security responsibilities, and maintain an agency-wide security program. The program includes assessments, controls, training, testing, remediation, incident handling, and continuity planning.

Testing at least annually

The frequency must reflect risk, with a statutory floor of at least annual testing and evaluation. This agency program requirement is separate from the independent evaluation in §3555.

Security-control testing at least annually

Statutory origin: FISMA legislation 2002 · Enacted 2002-11-25

GISRA already required periodic management testing. The 2002 provisions expressly set a floor of no less than annual testing, with more frequent testing where risk requires it. FISMA 2014 continued that rule.

The agency-wide security program

The statute specifies the program’s components. Risk assessments, policies, training, testing, remediation, incident response, and continuity planning form an ongoing program rather than a one-time authorization exercise.

An agency-wide information-security program

Statutory origin: GISRA 2000 · Enacted 2000-10-30

GISRA required an agency-wide program with risk assessments, policies, training, testing, remediation, and incident procedures. The 2002 laws expanded the program provisions; FISMA 2014 retained the program in §3554(b).
