44 USC §3520A — Chief Data Officer Council

The Chief Data Officer Council coordinates practices across agencies. Its membership and duties connect data management with statistical, evaluation, and information-policy work.

Statutory text

(a)

Establishment.—

There is established in the Office of Management and Budget a Chief Data Officer Council (in this section referred to as the “Council”).

Context and history: 44 USC 3520A(a)

(b)

Purpose and Functions.—

The Council shall—

establish Governmentwide best practices for the use, protection, dissemination, and generation of data;

(d)

Reports.—

The Council shall submit to the Director, the Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs of the Senate, and the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform of the House of Representatives a biennial report on the work of the Council.

(e)

Evaluation and Termination.—

(1)

GAO evaluation of council.—

Not later than 4 years after date 1

of the enactment of this section, the Comptroller General shall submit to Congress a report on whether the additional duties of the Council improved the use of evidence and program evaluation in the Federal Government.

(2)

Termination of council.—

The Council shall terminate and this section shall be repealed upon the expiration of the 2-year period that begins on the date the Comptroller General submits the report under paragraph (1) to Congress.
