40 USC §11312 — Capital planning and investment control

Agency heads establish capital-planning and investment-control processes for IT. These processes connect investments with budgets, performance, and management decisions.

Agencies

Title 40 recodification 2002P.L. 108-458

Section source laws · Explore its earlier history.

Statutory text

(a)

Design of Process.—

In fulfilling the responsibilities assigned under section 3506(h) of title 44, the head of each executive agency shall design and implement in the executive agency a process for maximizing the value, and assessing and managing the risks, of the information technology acquisitions of the executive agency.

Context and history: 40 USC 11312(a)

(b)

Content of Process.—

The process of an executive agency shall—

(1)

provide for the selection of investments in information technology (including information security needs) to be made by the executive agency, the management of those investments, and the evaluation of the results of those investments;
