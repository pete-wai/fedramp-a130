44 USC §3552 — Definitions

These definitions establish the reach of the security subchapter, including information security, federal information systems, national security systems, and binding operational directives. Definitions control the scope of the duties that follow.

Except as provided under subsection (b), the definitions under section 3502 shall apply to this subchapter.

Additional Definitions.—

As used in this subchapter:

The term “binding operational directive” means a compulsory direction to an agency that—

(A)

is for purposes of safeguarding Federal information and information systems from a known or reasonably suspected information security threat, vulnerability, or risk;

What makes a directive binding

A binding operational directive is a compulsory direction to an agency for safeguarding federal information and systems. Its scope depends on the rest of this subchapter, including the exceptions in §3553.
