44 USC §3607 — Definitions

FedRAMP has its own statutory vocabulary. Congress incorporates A-130’s definitions of authorization to operate and federal information, and NIST SP 800-145’s definition of cloud computing.

Statutory text

(a)

In General.—

Except as provided under subsection (b), the definitions under sections 3502 and 3552 apply to this section through section 3616.

In this section through section 3616:

The term “Administrator” means the Administrator of General Services.

Authorization to operate; federal information.—

The terms “authorization to operate” and “Federal information” have the meaning given those term 1

Congress incorporates A-130 terminology

The FedRAMP law adopts A-130’s definitions of authorization to operate and federal information, including successor documents. Here the statutory text expressly points to the circular.

A-130 definitions in the FedRAMP statute

(4)

Cloud computing.—

The term “cloud computing” has the meaning given the term in Special Publication 800–145 of the National Institute of Standards and Technology, or any successor document.

Context and history: 44 USC 3607(b)(4)

Congress incorporates NIST’s cloud definition

The law uses the cloud-computing definition in NIST SP 800-145 or its successor. The reference incorporates a definition; it does not make every NIST publication binding.

NIST’s cloud definition in statute

Statutory origin: FedRAMP Authorization Act 2022 · Enacted 2022-12-23

Congress adopted the cloud-computing definition from NIST SP 800-145 or its successor. The Special Publication itself was issued in 2011.
