44 USC §3512 — Public protection

The public generally cannot be penalized for failing to respond to a covered information collection that lacks a valid OMB control number or the required notice. This is a protection for respondents, not an exemption from agency security duties.

Statutory text

(a)

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no person shall be subject to any penalty for failing to comply with a collection of information that is subject to this subchapter if—

(1)

the collection of information does not display a valid control number assigned by the Director in accordance with this subchapter; or

Editorial Notes

Prior Provisions

A prior section 3512, added Pub. L. 96–511, § 2(a), Dec. 11, 1980, 94 Stat. 2822, related to protection of persons failing to maintain or provide information if information collection request did not display current control number prior to the general amendment of this chapter by Pub. L. 104–13.

Another prior section 3512, added Pub. L. 93–153, title IV, § 409(b), Nov. 16, 1973, 87 Stat. 593, related to information for independent regulatory agencies, prior to the general amendment of this chapter by Pub. L. 96–511.
