SUBJECT:  Designation of a Senior Agen cy Official for Geospatial Information 
 
To assist agencies and ensure consistency across the government, OMB is asking 
selected executive departments and agencies (see Attachment A for selected departments and 
agencies) to designate a senior agency official who has agency-wide responsibility, 
accountability, and authority for geospatial information issues.  Over the past several years, 
Federal agencies have better coordinated the government’s geospatial investments across 
agencies and with State, local, and tribal governments.  However, much work remains to identify 
and optimize Federal government investments.  Through further coordination, we will maximize 
our buying and maintaining of geospatial investments instead of independently investing in 
potentially duplicative and costly data and capabilities.   
 
Geospatial information (information associated with a specific geographic location) 
supports many missions including national security, law enforcement, health care, the 
environment, and natural resources conservation.  Examples of geospatial information include 
maps, satellite imagery, and census and housing data as well as information identified by a 
region or jurisdiction an agency may make available to other agencies, the public, or Congress.   
 
The Office of Management and Budget (OMB) has issued several policies relating to 
geospatial data, standards, and investments, including OMB Circulars A-11, A-16, A-119, and 
A-130.  In addition, Executive Order 12906 and the E-Government Act of 2002, section 216 
(Common Protocols for Geographic Information Systems), contain geospatial-related 
requirements.  Some agencies have successfully implemented these policies by designating one 
agency official to be responsible and accountable for geospatial activities and investments.  The 
Chief Information Officer may already perform this role in your agency.  Alternatively, you may 
designate another senior official (at the Assistant Secretary or equivalent level) for this role. 
 
The senior agency official will oversee, coordinate, and facilitate the agency’s 
implementation of the geospatial-related requirements, policies, and activities noted above.  
While these activities may be undertaken by various components of an agency, OMB will look 
to the senior agency official for information regarding the agency’s coordination of these 
activities.   

[PDF page 2]
Agencies responsible for spatial data themes are already required to be members of the 
Federal Geographic Data Committee (FGDC) pursuant to OMB Circular A-16.1  As a result of 
the senior agency official’s responsibility for internal coordination and implementation of 
geospatial-related initiatives and activities, the senior agency official will also serve as the 
policy-level official to represent each selected agency on the FGDC Steering Committee.   
 
Please send your designations for the senior agency official within 45 days of the date of 
this memorandum to Stacie Boyd, Government-to-Government Portfolio Manager, Office of 
E-Government and Information Technology, at Stacie_Boyd@omb.eop.gov or (202) 395-0342 
(fax).  Please provide the official’s name, title, and contact information (phone number and email 
address).  Ms. Boyd can also answer any questions your agency may have about this 
memorandum at (202) 395-0346.
