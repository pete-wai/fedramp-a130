# Reading interface

User-pinned direction: restrained research report, readable type, persistent section navigation, responsive tables and print styles. Mode: Read, with an embedded reference lookup task. A cream paper surface, dark ink, restrained blue links, serif headings and prose, sans-serif controls. Aligned chronology lanes distinguish law, OMB and NIST without implying causal arrows. Evidence opens inline; all narrative and records remain present in HTML without JavaScript. Mobile navigation becomes a compact horizontal list; the comparison table alone scrolls. No decorative imagery or motion.
